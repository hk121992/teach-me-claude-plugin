Meet your AI coworker

A two-minute read, then you'll try it yourself on the next slide.

What this thing actually is

The thing you're talking to isn't a chat box that just replies. It's an agent — a coworker that can open, read, create, and change real files for you, and take actions.

Three things it's not, so you set the right expectations:

  • not a search engine — it can sound completely sure and still be wrong
  • not a database of your private facts — it only knows what you show it
  • not something that learns from you as you chat

And it's one face of an engine that shows up elsewhere too (like Claude Code) — you're learning a coworker, not a single app.

What happens when you ask

You say
"create notes.md"
→
It does it
writes the real file
→
A real file appears
notes.md, in your folder

It doesn't describe making the file — it makes it. And that file is yours to open anytime, with or without the agent: your work lives in a real folder on your computer, not trapped inside the chat.

Lesson 1 · your activity

Catch it in the act

Send one instruction, then go check the file is really there — don't take the agent's word for it.

  1. Start a new task. In Cowork, click + New task at the top of the left sidebar — that opens a fresh, blank conversation. It won't remember this lesson, which is why your steps are in the box below to take with you.
  2. Point it at your project folder. Under the message box, open Work in a project or folder and pick your Teach Me Claude folder (series-01-foundations). Not sure it's set? Just ask the agent: Which folder are we working in right now?
  3. Send your first instruction — exactly this: Create a file called first-day.md and write today's date and my name at the top.
  4. Go find it yourself. Don't take its word that it's done — open first-day.md and see it's really there. That gap, between "I made the file" and seeing it, is the whole point.
  5. Now ask one small real thing. Something tiny from your actual work — Draft three subject lines for the email I have to send about … — and judge the result like the expert you are. Day one shouldn't end before it's helped you once for real.

Take these with you

You'll be in a new task, away from this page — keep these beside you (your guide will drop a link below to open them in your browser):

  • Click + New task (top-left) — a blank slate
  • Open Work in a project or folder → pick series-01-foundations
  • Send: "Create a file called first-day.md and write today's date and my name at the top."
  • Open first-day.md yourself and check it's really there
  • Ask one small real thing from your own work — then judge it
  • Hit a usage limit? It's a metered pause, not a broken course — your work is saved
