Catch it in the act

Keep this page beside you — the new task won't remember the lesson.

  1. Start a new task. In the Home tab, click + New at the top of the left sidebar (check Cowork is selected). It starts fresh: the agent has your folder and any global instructions you've set in Claude — nothing of this lesson or your guide. Working with that is part of the task.
  2. Point it at your project folder. Under the message box, click Project or folder and pick your Teach Me Claude folder for series 1 (series-01-foundations).
  3. Send your first instruction — exactly this: Create a file called first-day.md and write today's date and my name at the top.
  4. Go find it yourself. Don't take its word that it's done — open first-day.md and see it's really there.

Hit a usage limit? It's a metered pause, not a broken course — your work is saved. Wait for it to clear and pick up where you left off.

When you're done, come back to your home-base chat and tell your guide what happened.
