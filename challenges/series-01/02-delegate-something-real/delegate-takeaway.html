Lesson 2 · your activity steps

Hand over one real email

Keep this page beside you — the new task won't remember the lesson.

  1. Pick the email — pause first. With your guide, before you go: a real email you need to send. Anything in the material that's private, confidential, or not yours to share? Swap it for a stand-in — conservative for now; your own sharing policy comes later in the course.
  2. Start a new task. In the Home tab, click + New (check Cowork is selected), then point it at your series folder via Project or folder → series-01-foundations.
  3. Paste the context in. The thread you're answering, your notes, what it must say — the agent drafts from what you give it. No email access needed.
  4. Brief the job in your own words — who it's for, what it must accomplish, tone and length. Like briefing a capable new colleague on day one.
  5. Read before you approve. You may get a permission request when it acts on files — read it, say it back to yourself in plain words, then decide.
  6. Own the draft — version it. e.g. Save this as draft-v1 and start a draft-v2 — keep v1 untouched. Then ask for one bold revision in your own words — v1 makes it free. Compare, keep the best of both — pull back anything you liked from v1. You send the final yourself.

The first draft won't sound like you yet — that's normal; iterating it into your voice is part of the loop, not a failure of it.

Something went wrong? Recovery has three routes — undo, ask the agent to put it back, or go back to v1. v1 is the one that always works.

When you're done, come back to your home-base chat and tell your guide how it went.
